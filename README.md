# UK Co-Benefits Atlas — Datafest Exhibition Poster 2025

An interactive A0 exhibition poster visualising the co-benefits of climate actions and their monetised values across UK local authorities (2025–2050).

## What this shows

Climate action generates value beyond carbon reduction. This poster maps **11 co-benefit types** across **8 impact pathways** for **361 UK local authorities**, showing:

- **£145.6bn** total co-benefit value by 2050
- **Physical Activity Increase** accounts for 89% of positive value — health, not carbon, is the dominant story
- How benefits flow from co-benefit types (air quality, noise reduction, active travel) through to damage pathways (reduced mortality, NHS savings, QALY)
- Which local authorities generate the highest health impact values
- The trade-offs (longer travel times, congestion) sitting alongside the benefits

## Key finding

> Most climate benefits come from better health — not carbon alone. Increased physical activity and cleaner air account for the majority of monetised co-benefits, outweighing transport-related trade-offs such as longer travel times.

## About the Atlas

The UK Co-Benefits Atlas quantifies the wider societal benefits of climate action across all UK local authorities — going beyond carbon to capture health, wellbeing, and economic value from actions to reach Net Zero by 2050.

Values represent modelled estimates of wider socio-economic impacts. They are not direct financial savings or funding allocations. All values in £billions.
